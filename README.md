The unsent message to the woman who cuts my hair — "To: the woman who cuts my hair / You asked about my mother every month for six years, and I never told you she died before you ever cut my hair, because your chair was the only place anyone asked." A tiny Node 22 program that writes exactly that two-part confession to `/output/entry.txt`: six years of being asked about a mother who was already gone, and the one chair where someone still asks.

## Run it

```sh
~/workspace/makefun/node22/bin/node main.ts
```
